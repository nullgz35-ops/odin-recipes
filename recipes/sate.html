Sate

Chicken satay, or Sate Ayam, is a popular Indonesian dish made from marinated chicken pieces grilled on skewers. It is commonly served with peanut sauce and is known for its rich, savory flavor.

Ingredients:

  • 300 g chicken breast, cut into cubes
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sweet soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 10 bamboo skewers

For the Peanut Sauce:

  • 100g roasted peanuts
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 1 tablespoon sweet soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt 100 ml water

Tools:

  • Grill or frying pan
  • Mixing bowl
  • Knife
  • Cutting board
  • Blender or food processor
  • Spatula

Steps:

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine the chicken, garlic, soy sauce, sweet soy sauce, salt, black pepper, and cooking oil.
  2. Marinate the chicken for 15 to 30 minutes.
  3. Thread the chicken pieces onto the bamboo skewers.
  4. Grill the skewers over medium heat until the chicken is fully cooked and slightly charred.
  5. To make the peanut sauce, blend the roasted peanuts, garlic, sweet soy sauce, salt, and water until smooth.
  6. Cook the peanut sauce over low heat for 3 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Serve the grilled chicken satay with the warm peanut sauce.

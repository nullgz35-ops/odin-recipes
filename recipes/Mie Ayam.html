Mie Ayam

Chicken noodles, or Mie Ayam, is a popular Indonesian street food dish consisting of egg noodles topped with seasoned chicken, vegetables, and savory sauces. It is a comforting and flavorful meal enjoyed throughout Indonesia.

Ingredients:

  • 200 g egg noodles
  • 150 g chicken breast, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 green onion, sliced
  • 1 cup bok choy or green vegetables
  • 1 tablespoon sweet soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 cups chicken broth

Tools:

  • pot
  • frying pan
  • Spatula
  • Knife
  • Cutting board
  • Bowl

Steps:

  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil and cook the egg noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. In a wok or frying pan, heat the cooking oil over medium heat.
  3. Sauté the minced garlic until fragrant.
  4. Add the diced chicken and cook until fully done.
  5. Stir in the sweet soy sauce, soy sauce, salt, and black pepper.
  6. Cook for another 2 to 3 minutes until the chicken is well coated with the seasonings.
  7. Blanch the bok choy in boiling water for about 1 minute, then drain.
  8. Place the cooked noodles in a serving bowl.
  9. Top the noodles with the seasoned chicken and bok choy.
  10. Pour the hot chicken broth into the bowl or serve it on the side.
  11. Garnish with sliced green onions and serve immediately.
